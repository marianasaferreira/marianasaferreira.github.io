labase

labase is a Portuguese brand of minimalist jewelry, with an approach that combines elegance, simplicity and sophistication. Specializing in handcrafted pieces made from 925 silver, the brand is based on the tradition of Portuguese jewellery, while at the same time focusing on a modern and timeless aesthetic, creating pieces for everyday wear.

This project focused on carrying out a total rebranding of the brand with the aim of refining its visual identity so that it conveyed the brand’s philosophy while remaining true to its minimalist essence.

The rebranding included the reformulation of the logo, the creation of a new website, the development of packaging and publications for social media that mirrored the simplicity and sophistication of the brand. This process also aimed to strengthen labase’s presence in both the digital and physical markets.
