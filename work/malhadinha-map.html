Malhadinha Map

This project is a comprehensive visual exploration of Herdade da Malhadinha Nova, a luxury estate in the Alentejo region of Portugal, known for its commitment to sustainability, wine production, and rural tourism. This project consisted of an illustrated map that captures the essence of the estate while providing clarity, orientation, and aesthetic appeal for visitors.

The work combines custom cartography, iconographic design, and handcrafted illustrations to represent the estate’s diverse landscape and landmarks — including vineyards, villas, the olive grove, organic gardens, the equestrian center, and natural features like rivers and trails.

More than just a functional guide, this map invites discovery and reinforces Malhadinha’s brand values — authenticity, elegance, and a deep respect for nature. The final output was adapted for both print and digital use, enhancing the overall guest experience and supporting the brand’s visual identity in a meaningful and immersive way.
